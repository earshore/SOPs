邮件回复模板 SOP

已发布

常见客服场景的标准回复模板，确保响应及时、话术合规。

客服与客户体验体系 SLA: 24h内必回复

响应时效要求 (SLA)

所有买家消息必须在 24小时内 回复，超时将影响账号健康度。

禁止话术

严禁使用诱导好评的违规话术，如："如果您满意请给我们五星好评"、"留好评可获得返现"等。

Dear Customer,

Thank you for reaching out. We sincerely apologize for the delay in your shipment. We are actively working with our logistics partner to expedite the delivery.

Your order is expected to arrive within [X] days. We will keep you updated on the progress.

If you have any further questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.

Best regards,
[Brand Name] Customer Service
